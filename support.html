How can we help?

Rowing Weather is designed to be straightforward on the bank, in the launch and at the boathouse. These answers cover the most common questions.

Why does Rowing Weather need my location?

Location is used to centre the map near you and retrieve weather for the stretch of water you choose. You can also move the map and crosshair to inspect another location.

What is the Course Wind Analyser?

Set a course start and finish and Rowing Weather compares the forecast wind with the course bearing to show headwind, tailwind and crosswind components.

Is the rigging recommendation a fixed specification?

No. It is a practical starting point based on the inputs you choose. Always confirm measurements on the actual boat and test one change at a time on the water.

How do the subscriptions work?

Eligible new subscribers can receive a 7-day free trial. Monthly is €2.99 per month and yearly is €24.99 per year after the trial. Payment begins automatically when the trial ends and subscriptions renew until cancelled.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone or iPad open Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Rowing Weather and follow Apple’s cancellation options.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Rowing Weather Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Where are saved waters, courses and rigging setups stored?

They are stored locally on your device. You can delete saved items from their saved lists inside the app.
