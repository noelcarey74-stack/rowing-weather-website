Weather information

Forecasts and map information can change and may be delayed or inaccurate. Rowing Weather is a planning aid and should not replace local safety rules, official warnings, club procedures or a coach’s judgement of conditions on the water.

Rigging tool

Rigging outputs are suggested starting points. Boat, oar, crew and water conditions vary. Confirm measurements on the actual equipment and make changes carefully.

Subscriptions

Eligible new subscribers may receive a 7-day free trial. The monthly plan is €2.99 per month after the trial and the yearly plan is €24.99 per year after the trial. Payment starts automatically when the free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled and are managed through your Apple Account.
