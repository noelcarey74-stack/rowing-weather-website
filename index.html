BEFORE YOU PUSH OFF

A rowing forecast should tell you more than the wind speed.

Rowing Weather is designed around the questions rowers and coaches actually ask: What is the wind doing on our course? What will it feel like in the other direction? Is rain moving in? And what should we consider when setting up the boat?

GOOD TO KNOW

Before Rowing Weather launches.

Is Rowing Weather available on the App Store yet?

Not yet. The app is in the final pre-release stage. This website is a preview of the app and its current launch feature set.

Can I use anything without subscribing?

Yes. The Home screen is free and includes live weather at the map crosshair, animated wind, water pins, standard and satellite map views and quick tide lookup.

What does Rowing Weather Pro add?

Pro adds hourly and 7-day rowing forecasts, rain forecast tools, the Course Wind Analyser, saved waters and courses, the weather-aware Rigging Tool, saved rigging and PDF reports.

Is the rigging result a fixed specification?

No. It is a practical starting point. Always confirm measurements on the actual boat and test changes carefully on the water.
