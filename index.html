RIGGING TOOL

Weather and rigging in the same conversation.

Choose the boat, crew, target rate and blade. Rowing Weather combines those inputs with course wind to provide a clear starting setup and notes for testing.

The rigging tool is a coaching aid and starting point, not a replacement for crew feel, boat measurements or on-water testing.
