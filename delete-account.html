ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION

Delete your Rowing Weather Pro account

For the Android Rowing Weather Pro app, you can request deletion of your account and its associated cloud data, even if you have uninstalled the app.

How to request deletion

Email careywebservicesireland@gmail.com with the subject Rowing Weather Pro — Delete My Account. Include the email address you used to register. Do not send your password or payment details.

In the Android app you can also open More → Request account deletion to reach this page.

What happens next?

We may contact the registered email address to confirm account ownership. Once verified, we will delete the authentication account and associated server-side profile information, subject to any legally required retention. We will respond with confirmation or explain any necessary follow-up. Requests are handled within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days.

What about saved waters and courses?

Saved waters, courses and rigging setups in the Android app are stored locally on your device. Use More → Clear saved app data to remove them before uninstalling. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data on standard Android installations.

Subscriptions

Deleting an app account does not itself cancel Google Play subscriptions. If you purchased a subscription, cancel it separately in Google Play subscription settings. No Google Play purchases are enabled in the current Android testing preview.

See our privacy policy or support page for more information.
