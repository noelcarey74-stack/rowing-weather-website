PRIVACY POLICY

Your weather. Your water. Your privacy.

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

Location

Rowing Weather uses device location to centre the map and provide weather information for the area you choose. Location is used for app functionality. Rowing Weather does not use location for advertising or tracking.

Saved information

Saved waters, courses and rigging setups are stored on your device so you can reopen them later. The app does not require a Rowing Weather account.

Weather and mapping services

The app contacts weather and mapping services to retrieve forecasts, map information and related data for the coordinates you select. Those services may process technical request information under their own policies.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Rowing Weather does not receive or store your payment-card details.

Tracking and advertising

Rowing Weather does not use your location for targeted advertising and does not sell personal information.

Your control

You can change location permissions at any time in iPhone or iPad Settings. Saved waters, courses and rigging setups can be deleted inside the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Carey Web Services contact page.
