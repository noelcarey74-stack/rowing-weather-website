PRIVACY POLICY

Your weather. Your water. Your privacy.

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Who operates Rowing Weather?

Rowing Weather Pro is an independent rowing utility developed by Noel Carey / Carey Web Services. For support or privacy requests, email careywebservicesireland@gmail.com.

Location and forecasts

If you choose to grant location permission, your device's position helps centre the map and request local weather. You can also move the map manually. The coordinates you select are sent to weather and tide providers so forecasts can be returned. Those services and map-tile providers may receive standard technical request details such as your IP address. We do not use your location for advertising or tracking.

Saved rowing information

Saved waters, rowing courses and boat-rigging setups are stored on your device, and you can delete them in the app. Demonstration routes are identified as examples and are not official race-course charts. These local saved items are not synchronised to your Rowing Weather Pro account in the current Android version.

Accounts and sign-in

The iPhone and iPad version does not require an app account. The Android version permits optional email/password registration for account features. If you register, your email, encrypted authentication credentials and necessary account identifiers are processed by Supabase to create, confirm and maintain your sign-in, including password-recovery emails. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider; Rowing Weather does not ask you to send a password by email. Session tokens are stored encrypted on the Android device. The live weather map can be explored without registering.

Subscriptions and payments

The iPhone and iPad version uses Apple's App Store for purchases and subscriptions. Android Google Play subscription options are in development. If enabled, Google Play will process purchases and subscriptions. Rowing Weather does not receive or store your payment card number.

Advertising and analytics

We do not sell personal data and do not use your location for targeted advertising. We do not embed an advertising or cross-app tracking SDK in the Android preview. Platform services may process security and diagnostic data under their own policies.

Retention and your choices

You can change location permission in your device settings, delete local saved rowing items in the app, or sign out. To request deletion of an Android email account and its associated server-side data, use our Rowing Weather Pro account-deletion page or More → Request account deletion. After identity verification, account records will be deleted subject to lawful retention obligations. Any local data on your own device must be cleared separately.

Contact

For privacy questions and access or deletion requests, email careywebservicesireland@gmail.com. For general assistance see Support.
